Membership

Membership categories, fees, and how to join or renew.

Membership is not required to use ICB's services, but it is necessary to hold board office or to vote. Membership is open to anyone who agrees with the center's mission and meets the requirements below.

Regular Members

Must be a Muslim or the spouse of a Muslim, and at least 18 years old. Full voting rights.

Youth Members

A child of a regular member, 18 or older, and enrolled full-time in an educational program. Contact the Treasurer to apply, with your parents' membership information.

Junior Members

Children of regular members under 18 are automatically enrolled as junior members with full privileges and rights, except the right to vote or stand for office.
