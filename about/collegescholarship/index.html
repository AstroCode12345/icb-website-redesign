About ICB

College Scholarship

The ICB Wayland College Merit Scholarship, for graduating Sunday School students.

$5,000 for College

Merit and Community Service Scholarship · 2 scholarships available each year

This scholarship recognizes graduating Sunday School students who have shown outstanding academic achievement and exemplary service to the ICB community during their years at Sunday School.

Requirements
  • Starting college Fall 2027
  • Graduate of ICB Sunday School
  • Provided service to ICB (for example, TA or Youth Group)
  • Teacher recommendation and essay required
  • Interest in continuing to learn Islam or Arabic
