Our Services

What We Offer

Facilities Rental

Rates, rules, and the online booking form for renting our Social Hall for private events.

Wedding Services

Nikah ceremonies performed by volunteer Marriage Officiants registered with the Commonwealth.

Funeral Services

Guidance on funeral arrangements, washing and shrouding services, and our Muslim burial section at Forest Hills Cemetery.

Family Matters

Confidential support for families navigating parenting, relationships, mental health, and hardship.
Our Space

Around the Center

Main Prayer Hall

A large, carpeted prayer space used for all five daily prayers, Friday Jumu'ah, Eid prayers, and special congregational programs. Separate sections for men and women.

Social Hall

A spacious multi-purpose hall available for community dinners, events, lectures, and celebrations. Maximum seating capacity of 160. Equipped with tables, chairs, and a kitchen area.

Classrooms

Dedicated classroom spaces used by the Sunday Islamic School for K–10 instruction, small group learning, and youth programming.

Wudu Facilities

Dedicated ablution (wudu) facilities for both men and women, conveniently located near the prayer hall entrance.

Parking

On-site parking is available for worshippers and visitors. Additional street parking is accessible on nearby roads during high-attendance events.
