Nikah ceremonies performed by volunteer Marriage Officiants.

Nikkah (Wedding) Ceremony

All our services are performed by volunteers. Accordingly, we have volunteers who can perform Nikah ceremonies. They have registered with the State of Massachusetts as Marriage Officiants. However, it is the responsibility of the couples interested in getting married to complete the city and state required paperwork directly with those agencies and departments.

To check availability, email nikah@icbwayland.org with the names of both people getting married, the proposed date, time, and location, and your contact details. A volunteer will respond to coordinate.
