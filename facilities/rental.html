Facilities

Facilities Rental

Rates, rules, and online booking for private events at ICB.

ICB facilities including the prayer hall, social hall and kitchen are available for use for private events that do not conflict with normal activities of the center. All events are subject to review by the President, Board of Directors, or a committee established by the Board to review such requests. The Board reserves the right to reject any request.

Facilities Reservation:

The procedure to reserve the facility is as follows:

1.

First check the ICB Event Calendar for availability. The calendar will open in a new tab.

2.

Read details about types of events, associated fees, rules and regulations in the Rental Policy, Fees, Agreement and Online Booking Form for ICB Facilities. The last page of this document has the link to ICB's Online Facility Booking Form.

3.

Once you have filled the form, an automatic form submission email will be sent to the email address you have provided. Make sure to fill it correctly.

4.

Contact us at facilities@icbwayland.org if you do not hear back from us within 7 days of submitting your form.

Payment & Policy

What to Know Before You Book

Payment

Once you submit the form, ICB reviews the request and sends a confirmation email with a link to pay a 50% deposit, due within 2 days. The remaining 50% (and any staffing fee) is due one day before the event.

Cancellation

Cancel at least three weeks before your event for a full refund, or reschedule once at no cost. Full refunds apply for weather emergencies or facility issues beyond your control.

Hours & Conduct

Events are limited to five hours and must conclude by 11 PM. All events must adhere to commonly accepted Islamic traditions. No shoes, food, or decorations in the prayer hall. No smoking, pets, or open flames.
