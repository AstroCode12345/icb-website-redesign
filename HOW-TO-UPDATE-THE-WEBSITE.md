# How to Update the ICB Wayland Website

This guide is for anyone at the mosque who needs to change something on the website.
You do not need to know how to code. Everything is done through a web page with
normal boxes you type into.

---

## Getting in

1. Open the admin website link in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or a phone browser).
2. Type the admin password.
3. Press **Sign In**.

You will see a menu down the left side and the editing boxes in the middle.

> **Keep the password private.** Anyone who has it can change the website.

---

## The one rule to remember

Nothing you type appears on the real website until you press the green
**Publish Changes** button.

The button is at the top right of the screen and again at the very bottom.
You can change several things and press Publish once at the end.

After you press Publish, wait about 30 seconds, then refresh the website to see it.

---

## What you can change

The menu on the left has six sections.

### 1. Prayer Times

The five daily Iqamah times, plus the Friday Khutbah and Iqamah times.

Type times exactly like this: `5:00 AM` or `10:05 PM`
(number, colon, two digits, a space, then AM or PM)

There is also a **Last Updated** box. Put the current month and year there,
for example `July 2026`, so visitors know the schedule is current.

### 2. Friday Speaker

The name of this week's Jumu'ah speaker and the date.
Update this each week before Friday.

### 3. Announcement Banner

A yellow bar that appears at the very top of every page.
Use it for short, important notices, for example:

- "Eid prayer will be held at 8:00 AM on Sunday."
- "The mosque will be closed Saturday for maintenance."

Turn the switch **on** to show it, and **off** when the notice no longer applies.

> Turn the banner off when it is no longer needed. A stale notice looks worse
> than no notice.

### 4. Events

Everything the community sees under Upcoming Events on the home page and the
calendar page.

To add an event, press **Add Event** and fill in the boxes:

| Box | What to type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Month / Label | The short month | `May` |
| Day | The day number | `9` |
| Category | The type of event | `Charity` |
| Event Title | The name of the event | `First Annual Humanitarian Walk` |
| Details | Time, place, and any notes | `Saturday · 10:00 AM · Open to all` |

Tick **Featured** to highlight one important event in gold. Use this sparingly,
usually for just one event at a time.

To remove an event that has already happened, press **Remove** on it.

> The Category box also controls the filter buttons on the calendar page.
> If you type `Charity`, that event shows up when someone clicks the Charity filter.

### 5. Sunday School

The Zuhr prayer time held during Sunday school. Changing it here updates it on the
Prayers page, the School page, and the Calendar page at the same time.

### 6. Contact & Social

- The contact email address used in the "Contact Us" links
- The Facebook page address
- The YouTube channel address

Only change these if the mosque's email or social accounts actually change.

### Donation Link

The web address the Donate buttons point to.
Only change this if the mosque switches to a different donation service.

---

## Things this tool does automatically

You do not need to do anything for these:

- **The countdown to the next prayer** in the green bar updates itself.
- **The small calendar** on the calendar page always shows the correct month
  and marks today's date.
- **The date on the events** and the layout of the pages take care of themselves.

---

## If something looks wrong

**I pressed Publish but the website looks the same.**
Wait about 30 seconds and refresh the page. On a phone, close the tab and open it
again. If it still looks old, try again on a different browser.

**I made a mistake and published it.**
Just fix the box and press Publish again. The correction goes live the same way.
Nothing is permanently broken.

**It says the publish failed.**
Press Publish once more. If it keeps failing, the connection to the storage service
may need attention. Contact whoever set the site up.

**I forgot the password.**
The password cannot be recovered from inside the tool. Whoever set the site up can
set a new one.

---

## A short checklist for weekly updates

- [ ] Update the Friday speaker name and date
- [ ] Remove any event that has already happened
- [ ] Add any new events
- [ ] Turn off the announcement banner if that notice is finished
- [ ] Check the prayer times still match the current season
- [ ] Press **Publish Changes**

---

## What this tool does not change

The wording of the regular pages (About, Programs, Outreach, Resources), the
photographs, the colours, and the page layouts are not editable here. Those need
someone comfortable editing the website files.

Everything that changes regularly at a mosque, prayer times, the Friday speaker,
events, and announcements, is covered by this tool.
