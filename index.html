About ICB

Established 1979

The Islamic Center of Boston (ICB) was established in 1979 (1400 AH) as a nonprofit, nonsectarian religious, educational and cultural organization. Services provided by the ICB include congregational prayers, Ramadan iftaars and taraveeh, a Sunday school, eid programs, educational lectures, and interfaith activities.
