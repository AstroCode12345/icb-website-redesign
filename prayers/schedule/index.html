Prayer Schedule for Boston

Daily prayer times through the year for the Boston area.

These are not the congregation times at ICB. The table below gives the astronomical prayer times for Boston. For the times the congregation actually stands for prayer at the mosque, see the ICB Iqamah times.

Boston, MA: Latitude = 42° 21′ North and Longitude = 71° 03′ West. Qibla = 60° 13′ East of North.

* One hour has been added to times between March 15 and November 5 to account for daylight saving time. The actual start and end dates for daylight saving time vary each year. It starts on the second Sunday of March and ends on the first Sunday of November.
