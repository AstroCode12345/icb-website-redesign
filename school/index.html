Education

Sunday Islamic School

Faith-centered education for grades K through 10.

Our Mission

Rooted in Faith, Ready for the World

Sunday school runs September through June for students in grades K–10. Classes are small, discussion-based, and taught by dedicated teachers from our community.

Subject 1

Islamic Arabic

Students learn to read and recite Arabic, building a connection to the Quran through memorization and translation.

Curriculum Overview (PDF) →
Subject 2

Religion

Students explore Islamic history, beliefs, and daily practice through discussion and group activities.

Schedule & Access

School Details

Schedule

Every Sunday, 10:30 AM – 4:30 PM, September through June. Each student attends two weekly classes: Islamic Arabic and Religion. Zuhr prayer is held at 12:30 PM for all students and families.

Who Can Enroll

Open to students in grades K through 10. Families do not need to be ICB members to enroll, though membership is encouraged.
